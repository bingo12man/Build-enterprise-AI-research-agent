Artificial intelligence can improve manufacturing operations in several areas.

Predictive maintenance uses historical maintenance records and equipment sensor data to identify patterns associated with machine failure. Early detection of abnormal behavior can help maintenance teams inspect equipment before an unexpected breakdown occurs.

Computer vision can be used in production lines to inspect products for visual defects. Automated inspection systems can improve consistency and reduce the amount of repetitive manual inspection required.

Demand forecasting can help manufacturers estimate future product demand and improve inventory planning. Forecast accuracy depends heavily on historical data quality and changing market conditions.

Successful AI adoption requires reliable data collection, integration with existing systems, monitoring of model performance, and clear human oversight.
